Sunlight = Energy (Photons)

Sooraj ki roshni mein energy particles hote hain jise photons kehte hain.Ye photons solar panel par girte hi process shuru hoti hai.

Solar Cells (Silicon) Electrons Ko Hilate Hain

Solar panel ke andar silicon cells hote hain.Jab photons silicon ko hit karte hain to electrons apni jagah se hilne lagte hain.

Electrons Ki Movement = DC Electricity

Electrons ek direction mein flow karte hain.Is flow ko hi DC current (Direct Current) kehte hain.

Metal Grid Current Ko Collect Karta Hai

Solar cell ke upar chhoti-chhoti metal lines (grid) hoti hain.Ye saare electrons ko collect karke wires tak bhej deti hain.

Inverter DC Ko AC Mein Badalta Hai

Ghar ke saare appliances AC current par chalte hain.Isliye inverter DC ko pure AC mein convert karta hai.

Electricity Ghar, Battery, Grid Sabko Jaati Hai

Ghar mein supply hota hai, battery charge karta hai (agar hybrid/off-grid system ho), grid mein bheja jata hai (agar on-grid system ho).

ON Grid Solar System

An On-Grid Solar System is a solar power setup that is directly connected to the public electricity grid. It allows users to generate electricity from solar panels during the day and use grid power when solar generation is insufficient, ensuring a continuous and reliable power supply.

Read More

Off-Grid Solar Power System

An Off-Grid Solar Power System operates independently of the public electricity grid, making it an ideal solution for remote areas and locations with unreliable or no grid access. This system generates electricity using solar panels and stores excess energy in batteries for use during nighttime or cloudy conditions.

Read More

Solar Submersible Pump

A Solar Submersible Pump is an energy-efficient water pumping solution powered by solar energy, designed especially for agricultural, residential, and rural water supply applications. The pump operates using electricity generated from solar panels, eliminating dependence on grid.

Read More

Solar Water Heater

A Solar Water Heater is an eco-friendly system that uses sunlight to heat water for domestic, commercial, and industrial use. It captures solar energy through collectors and transfers the heat to water stored in an insulated tank, providing hot water without relying on electricity or fossil fuels.
